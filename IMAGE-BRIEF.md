# Image brief: bright, colourful homepage images

The client wants every image to look like `src/assets/images/pickup-team.webp` (used
in "Why choose SKV"): bright, clean, colourful, SKV green, on a white background or in
a well-lit scene.

Generate each image with the **shared style** below plus that slot's **subject**.
Save it under the given filename, at the given shape, at least 1600px on the long
side, and send it back. Each file replaces the current photo directly.

The hero (`hero-circuit-boards.webp`) is client-approved and is **not** on this list.

## Shared style (paste before every subject)

> Bright, high-key commercial photo, clean and colourful, crisp detail, soft daylight,
> fresh green (#0a6a22 / #3d9f25) and white colour scheme with small touches of
> yellow, Indian staff where people appear, wearing dark-green caps and polo shirts
> with yellow high-visibility vests and gloves, tidy and professional, no visible
> brand names, no text other than "E-WASTE" or a recycling symbol, no logos.

Add **"isolated on a pure white background"** to the slots marked *cut-out*.

## Rules

- No fake SKV signage, certificates, client logos or certificate numbers in any image.
- These are illustrations, not photos of SKV's real sites. Sections that show them
  keep the small "Representative image" label.
- Keep the same faces, uniforms and truck across images so the set feels like one shoot.
